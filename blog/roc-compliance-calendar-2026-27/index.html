ROC compliance calendar 2026-27: every MCA due date for companies and LLPs

ROCNovember 2026Vitt-Marg Advisors

Most ROC penalties are not caused by difficult law. They are caused by a date nobody wrote down. This page lists every recurring MCA filing for a private company or LLP with a 31 March year-end, the date it falls due, and what changed in 2026.

Extensions in force as of 4 November 2026. For FY 2025-26, MCA General Circular 05/2026 lets companies file AOC-4 up to 31 December 2026 and MGT-7 / MGT-7A up to 31 January 2027 without additional fee. General Circular 06/2026 does the same for CRA-4 up to 31 December 2026. These circulars waive the late fee only. They do not extend the AGM date.

LLP filings

  • Form 11 (annual return): by 30 May. For FY 2025-26 it was due 30 May 2026.
  • Form 8 (statement of account and solvency): by 30 October. For FY 2025-26 it was due 30 October 2026, and no extension was announced.

The general MCA circulars on AOC-4 and MGT-7 do not cover LLP forms, so an LLP that has missed 30 October is already paying additional fee. See our note on LLP Form 8 and Form 11 late fees.

Income-tax dates that sit next to the ROC calendar

  • Tax audit report for FY 2025-26: extended to 21 October 2026 by CBDT Circular 07/2026.
  • Income-tax return for audited companies and firms: 21 November 2026, or 30 November 2026 where a transfer pricing report is needed.
  • Advance tax third instalment: 15 December 2026.

What happens if you miss them

  • AOC-4 and MGT-7: ₹100 a day per form as additional fee, with no upper limit, counted from the original due date once the relief window closes.
  • Other forms (ADT-1, MGT-14, DIR-12, DPT-3 and similar): additional fee in slabs, starting at one times the normal fee and rising with the delay.
  • Penalties under Sections 92 and 137 are separate from the fee and can reach ₹2 lakh for the company and ₹50,000 for each officer in default.
  • Director disqualification under Section 164(2) if financial statements or annual returns are not filed for three consecutive years, and possible strike-off of the company.

The full fee maths is in our note on AOC-4 and MGT-7 late fees.

Frequently asked questions

What is the last date for AOC-4 for FY 2025-26?

AOC-4 is normally due within 30 days of the AGM. For FY 2025-26, MCA General Circular 05/2026 allows filing without additional fee up to 31 December 2026.

What is the last date for MGT-7 for FY 2025-26?

MGT-7 and MGT-7A are normally due within 60 days of the AGM. For FY 2025-26 they can be filed without additional fee up to 31 January 2027.

Is the AGM date also extended?

No. The 2026 circulars waive the additional fee for the forms only. The AGM was still due by 30 September 2026, and holding it late is a separate default.

Do I need to file DIR-3 KYC every year?

No. From 31 March 2026, DIR-3 KYC is due once every three financial years, by 30 June. Directors who were already compliant next file by 30 June 2028. Changes in mobile, email or address must still be reported within 30 days.

Which ROC office handles Noida companies?

Companies registered in Noida, Greater Noida and Ghaziabad come under the Registrar of Companies, Kanpur.
