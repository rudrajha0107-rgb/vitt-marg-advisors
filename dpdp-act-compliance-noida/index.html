Does the DPDP Act apply to your business?

Yes, if you collect or use the personal data of people in India in digital form. That covers customer details on your website or app, employee records, vendor contacts, CCTV footage stored digitally, and data you share with payroll, CRM or marketing tools. Size does not matter: a 20-person company is a "data fiduciary" just like a large one.

DateWhat happens
14 November 2025DPDP Rules, 2025 notified; the Data Protection Board is set up
November 2026Registration of consent managers opens (12 months after notification)
May 2027Core obligations apply (18 months after notification): privacy notice, consent, security safeguards, breach intimation, children's data and data erasure

Penalties under the Act go up to ₹250 crore for failing to take reasonable security safeguards, and up to ₹200 crore for not reporting a personal data breach. Most companies need several months to get ready, so starting now is sensible.

What we do

  • Data mapping: what personal data you collect, why, where it is stored, who can access it and which vendors receive it.
  • Gap assessment: your current practices tested against the Act and the Rules, with a prioritised list of gaps. See our gap assessment service.
  • Privacy notice and consent: a clear notice in plain language, consent capture and withdrawal on your website, app and forms, and records that prove consent.
  • Security safeguards: access control, encryption, logging and backups reviewed and documented, with VAPT where your systems need testing.

  • Vendor contracts: data processing clauses for payroll, CRM, cloud and marketing vendors who handle personal data for you.
  • Grievance contact and team training: a published contact for data requests and a short training for staff who handle personal data.

Frequently asked questions

Does the DPDP Act apply to small businesses?

Yes. Any business that processes personal data of people in India in digital form is a data fiduciary under the Act, whatever its size. Customer, employee and vendor data all count.

When do the DPDP Rules 2025 come into force?

The Rules were notified on 14 November 2025. Consent manager registration follows after 12 months, and the core obligations such as notice, consent, security safeguards and breach intimation apply 18 months after notification, around May 2027.

What is the penalty under the DPDP Act?

Penalties go up to ₹250 crore for failing to take reasonable security safeguards and up to ₹200 crore for not reporting a personal data breach, with other amounts for other failures. The Data Protection Board decides the penalty case by case.

Do we need a Data Protection Officer?

A Data Protection Officer based in India is mandatory for Significant Data Fiduciaries notified by the government. Other businesses must still publish a contact who answers questions about personal data.

How long does DPDP readiness take?

For most small and mid-sized companies, the data mapping, gap assessment and main fixes take 4 to 8 weeks. Larger companies with many systems and vendors need longer.
