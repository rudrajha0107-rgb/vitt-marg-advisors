How to add or remove a partner in GST registration, and change the authorised signatory

GSTNovember 2026By CMA Sudarshan Rudra Jha

A new partner has joined your firm. Or an old partner has retired. Or the person who used to sign your GST returns has left, and someone else will handle it now. In all these cases, your GST registration has to be updated.

The rule is simple: you should apply for the change within 15 days of the date it happened. Many businesses forget, and the problem shows up later when a notice or refund gets stuck because the details on the GST portal are old.

Partner change vs signatory change: what is the difference?

The GST portal splits changes into two types.

  • Core field changes need approval from the GST officer. Adding or removing a partner is a core change. So is a change in the business name or the main business address.
  • Non-core field changes are approved automatically once you verify with OTP. Changing the authorised signatory, the mobile number, the email or the bank account is a non-core change.

How to add or remove a partner in GST

  1. Get the paperwork right first. For a new partner, sign a supplementary partnership deed. For a retiring partner, sign a retirement deed. All partners sign it.
  2. Log in to the GST portal and go to Services, then Registration, then Amendment of Registration Core Fields.
  3. Open the Promoter / Partners tab. Add the new partner's details, or delete the partner who left.
  4. Enter the date of the change and the reason, and upload the deed.
  5. Submit with DSC or EVC. The GST officer checks it. If the officer has a question, it comes on the portal and you reply there. If the officer does not act in time, the change is approved automatically.

How to change the authorised signatory in GST

  1. Prepare a letter of authorisation signed by the partners, naming the new person. For a company, this is a board resolution.
  2. Go to Amendment of Registration Non-Core Fields and open the Authorised Signatory tab.
  3. Add the new person. If the old person was the main (primary) signatory, first make the new person primary, then remove the old one. The portal will not let you remove the primary signatory without putting someone in their place.
  4. The new signatory verifies their mobile number and email with OTP. That is it. The change is approved straight away.

Documents you will need

  • Supplementary deed or retirement deed, signed by all partners
  • PAN and Aadhaar of the new partner or new signatory
  • Address proof and a passport-size photo of the new partner
  • Letter of authorisation or board resolution for the new signatory

Mistakes we see often

  • Applying months after the change. Stick to the 15 days.
  • Removing the old primary signatory before adding the new one, which the portal blocks.
  • Updating GST but forgetting the bank, the income tax portal and Udyam registration.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a new partner in GST registration?

Sign a supplementary partnership deed, then file a core field amendment on the GST portal under Amendment of Registration Core Fields, add the partner in the Promoter / Partners tab and upload the deed. The GST officer approves it.

How do I remove a partner from GST registration?

Sign a retirement deed, then file a core field amendment, delete the partner in the Promoter / Partners tab, give the date and reason, and upload the deed.

How do I change the authorised signatory in GST?

File a non-core amendment, add the new person in the Authorised Signatory tab, make them primary if needed, and complete OTP verification. It is approved automatically.

What is the time limit to update GST registration?

You should apply within 15 days of the change.

Is a change of partner a core or non-core amendment in GST?

Adding or removing a partner is a core amendment and needs approval from the GST officer. Changing the authorised signatory is non-core.
