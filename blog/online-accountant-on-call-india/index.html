Online accountant on call for small businesses anywhere in India: how it works

On-CallNovember 2026Vitt-Marg Advisors

Your books do not need someone in the same city. Most accounting work today happens in Tally, Zoho Books or Busy, and on the GST and income tax portals. A good accountant can do it on a video call while you share your screen, and you watch every entry being made.

With the Vitt-Marg On-Call Accountant, you can book one of our accountants on a call from anywhere in India, for 1.5, 2 or 3 hours.

Book now: tap the On-Call Accountant button with our photo at the bottom-right of this page, choose a call or an office visit and the hours, and we confirm your slot. Or message us on WhatsApp.

How a call session works

  1. Choose "On a call", pick the hours and the work, and share your name and mobile number.
  2. We confirm the slot and send a Google Meet or Zoom link, or simply call you.
  3. You share your screen, or give the accountant a separate login to your accounting software. You stay in control.
  4. At the end you get a WhatsApp summary of what was done, checked by our CA or CMA.

Is it safe?

  • Our accountants are salaried employees of Vitt-Marg, not anonymous freelancers.
  • Create a separate user in Tally or Zoho for the session and remove it after, or simply share your screen.
  • Never share bank passwords or OTPs. You log in to the GST or income tax portal yourself; we guide you.

Work that is ideal for a call

  • GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and GSTR-2B matching
  • Bank reconciliation and ledger clean-up
  • TDS challans and quarterly returns
  • Understanding a GST or income tax notice before you reply
  • Month-end closing and a simple profit and cash summary
