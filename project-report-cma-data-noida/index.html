What the bank asks for

  • Project report: the business, promoters, product, market, cost of project, means of finance, and projected profit and cash flow for the loan period.
  • CMA data: the standard Credit Monitoring Arrangement format banks use to assess working capital limits.
  • Supporting statements: repayment schedule, depreciation schedule, break-even and sensitivity analysis.

The six CMA forms

FormContent
Form IExisting and proposed banking limits
Form IIOperating statement: sales, costs and profit for past, current and projected years
Form IIIAnalysis of the balance sheet
Form IVComparative statement of current assets and current liabilities, with holding periods
Form VComputation of the maximum permissible bank finance (MPBF)
Form VIFund flow statement

The ratios a banker checks first

RatioWhat banks commonly look for
Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR)Average of 1.25 to 1.5 or more over the loan period
Current ratioAround 1.33 or more for working capital limits
Total outside liabilities to tangible net worthUsually 3 to 4 or less, depending on the bank and sector
Interest coverage and break-evenComfortable margin above break-even in the first full year

For example, if a business expects a net profit of ₹12 lakh, depreciation of ₹5 lakh and term loan interest of ₹4.5 lakh, against yearly loan instalments of ₹10 lakh, its DSCR is (12 + 5 + 4.5) ÷ (4.5 + 10), which is about 1.48. That is a comfortable number for most banks.

Why a CMA-prepared report holds up

  • Projections start from a cost per unit, not a guessed profit percentage.
  • Capacity use is realistic: a new plant rarely runs at full capacity in its first year, and banks know it.
  • Working capital is worked out from real holding periods for stock and debtors, which is what the bank tests in Form IV.
  • Every assumption is written down, so the banker's questions have ready answers.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a project report take?

A standard project report with CMA data usually takes five to seven working days once we have your past financials, quotations for machinery and basic business details.

Can you make the projections look better so the loan is approved?

We present your business in its best honest light, but the numbers must be achievable. Inflated projections get questioned by the bank and become a problem later when actual results are compared with them at renewal.

Do you prepare stock statements for our cash credit limit?

Yes. We prepare monthly or quarterly stock and book debt statements and the drawing power calculation, and can issue a CA certificate where the bank needs one.

Our bank has its own CMA format. Can you use it?

Yes. Most banks use a version of the same six forms. We fill the bank's own template where they insist on it.
