Cost Accounting Standards CAS-1 to CAS-24: the full list and a cost records checklist

Cost AuditNovember 2026Vitt-Marg Advisors

Cost records are not a separate set of books. They are your financial books, stock records and production data, organised product by product according to the Cost Accounting Standards issued by the Institute of Cost Accountants of India. Your cost auditor checks the records against these standards, so knowing what each one covers saves a lot of back-and-forth at audit time.

The short version. There are 24 Cost Accounting Standards. Each tells you how to measure and assign one element of cost, such as material, labour, utilities or overheads. Keep your records in Form CRA-1 every month, not at year-end, and the audit becomes a review rather than a rebuild.

What Form CRA-1 expects you to keep

  • Material records: receipts, issues and closing stock by item, with the valuation method used.
  • Employee cost: wages, salaries and benefits, assigned to departments and products.
  • Utilities: power, fuel and water consumed, by cost centre.
  • Production records: quantity produced, scrap, wastage and yield for each product.
  • Overheads: collected by cost centre and absorbed into products on a reasonable basis.
  • A reconciliation of the profit as per cost records with the profit as per the financial accounts.

Cost records must be kept for at least eight financial years.

A monthly checklist that keeps you audit-ready

  1. Close the stock register and reconcile physical stock with book stock for key items.
  2. Record production quantity, rejections and scrap for each product line.
  3. Book power, fuel and water against the right cost centres, not one general account.
  4. Allocate wages to departments using attendance or time records.
  5. Check that capacity utilisation is being tracked, because abnormal idle capacity cost must be kept out of product cost under CAS-2.
  6. Prepare a one-page cost sheet per product and compare it with the selling price.
  7. At the quarter-end, reconcile cost-record profit with the financial accounts and note the differences.

Why this is more than compliance

Well-kept cost records show you which product actually makes money, where material yield is slipping, and how much idle capacity is costing you. Frequently asked questions

How many Cost Accounting Standards are there?

There are 24 Cost Accounting Standards, CAS-1 to CAS-24, issued by the Institute of Cost Accountants of India.

In which form are cost records maintained?

Cost records are maintained in Form CRA-1 under the Companies (Cost Records and Audit) Rules, 2014.

How long must cost records be kept?

Cost records must be preserved for at least eight financial years.

Which standard deals with capacity?

CAS-2 covers capacity determination, including installed, normal and actual capacity and the treatment of abnormal idle capacity cost.
