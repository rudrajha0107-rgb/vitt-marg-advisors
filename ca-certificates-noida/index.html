Foreign remittances: Form 145 and Form 146

From 1 April 2026, under the Income-tax Act, 2025, Form 15CA is now Form 145 and Form 15CB is now Form 146. The purpose is the same.

  • Form 145 is the remitter's declaration, filed online before the bank sends the money.
  • Form 146 is the Chartered Accountant's certificate. It confirms the nature of the payment, whether tax is due in India, the rate under the Act or the tax treaty, and the tax deducted.
  • A CA certificate is generally needed when the payment is taxable in India and the total sent to that person is more than ₹5 lakh in the financial year. Some payment types, such as many personal remittances under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme, do not need the forms at all.

The certificate is where most mistakes happen: the wrong treaty rate, a missing Tax Residency Certificate, or a service treated as non-taxable when it is not. We check the contract and the treaty before we sign, which protects you if the remittance is questioned later.

Net worth certificates for visas

Embassies want to see that you or your sponsor can support the trip. We prepare the certificate from your bank balances, fixed deposits, investments, property and loans, with property at a fair value supported by documents. We can issue it in the format the embassy or university asks for, and in foreign currency where needed.

What we need from you

  • Foreign remittance: invoice or agreement, the payee's Tax Residency Certificate and declaration if treaty benefit is claimed, and the bank details.
  • Net worth: PAN, bank statements, investment statements, property papers and loan statements.
  • Turnover: audited accounts or the latest GST returns, and the format required by the tender or bank.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can I get a CA certificate?

Most net worth, turnover and income certificates are ready within one to two working days once the documents are complete. Form 146 for a routine payment is usually done the same or next working day.

How can the receiver check that the certificate is genuine?

Every certificate carries a UDIN, a unique number generated on the ICAI portal. The bank, embassy or tender authority can verify it online.

Is Form 15CB still valid?

For remittances from 1 April 2026, the forms under the Income-tax Act, 2025 apply, so Form 146 replaces 15CB and Form 145 replaces 15CA. The content is very similar.

Can an NRI get Form 146 for sending money from an NRO account?

Yes. NRIs can move up to USD 1 million a financial year from their NRO account, after tax on the income has been paid. We check the source and the tax position and issue the certificate the bank needs.
