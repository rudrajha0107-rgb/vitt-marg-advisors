Appointing a cost auditor: who is eligible, how the fee is approved, and filing CRA-2

Cost AuditNovember 2026Vitt-Marg Advisors

Once cost audit applies, the first formal step is appointing the cost auditor. It looks like a routine Board resolution, but small slips here, such as the wrong person, a missed ratification or a late CRA-2, are among the most common cost audit defaults we see.

The short version. The cost auditor must be a cost accountant in practice (a CMA with a certificate of practice) or a firm of cost accountants, and cannot be your statutory auditor. The Board appoints within 180 days from the start of the year, shareholders ratify the fee, and CRA-2 is filed within 30 days of the Board meeting.

Who can be appointed

  • A cost accountant in practice, that is, a member of the Institute of Cost Accountants of India holding a valid certificate of practice, or a firm of such cost accountants.
  • Not your statutory auditor. Section 148(3) bars a person appointed as auditor under Section 139 from conducting the company's cost audit.
  • Not a disqualified person. The disqualifications that apply to statutory auditors also apply to cost auditors, so far as they can. For example, an officer or employee of the company, a person who is indebted to the company beyond the prescribed limit, or a person with a disqualifying business relationship cannot be appointed.

The appointment process, step by step

  1. Get the consent and eligibility certificate from the proposed cost auditor, confirming that they are not disqualified.
  2. Audit Committee recommendation. If the company must have an Audit Committee, the committee recommends the person and the fee to the Board.
  3. Board resolution. The Board appoints the cost auditor and approves the fee, within 180 days from the start of the financial year.
  4. Intimate the cost auditor of the appointment in writing.
  5. File Form CRA-2 with the MCA within 30 days of the Board meeting, or within 180 days from the start of the year, whichever is earlier.
  6. Shareholders ratify the fee by an ordinary resolution at a general meeting, usually the next AGM.

Casual vacancy, resignation and removal

  • Casual vacancy. If the cost auditor resigns, dies or is removed, the Board must fill the vacancy within 30 days, and the company must file CRA-2 for the new appointment within 30 days of it.
  • Removal before the term ends. The Board can remove a cost auditor only after giving a reasonable opportunity of being heard and recording its reasons in writing. The CRA-2 for the new cost auditor must carry the details of the removal.
  • Term. A cost auditor continues until 180 days from the end of the financial year, or until the report is submitted.

Practical tips

  • Pass the Board resolution early in the year, not in September. It gives the cost auditor time to review records quarterly instead of all at once.
  • Keep the CRA-2 SRN with the auditor's appointment letter. CRA-4 will later ask for these details and they must match.
  • Remember that the statutory auditor and the cost auditor are different people, so plan both appointments together at the start of the year.

Frequently asked questions

Who can be appointed as a cost auditor?

Only a cost accountant in practice, that is, a member of the Institute of Cost Accountants of India with a certificate of practice, or a firm of such cost accountants.

Can the statutory auditor also be the cost auditor?

No. Section 148(3) of the Companies Act, 2013 bars a person appointed as auditor under Section 139 from conducting the cost audit of the same company.

What is the time limit for filing CRA-2?

CRA-2 must be filed within 30 days of the Board meeting that appointed the cost auditor, or within 180 days from the start of the financial year, whichever is earlier.

Who approves the cost auditor's fee?

The Board approves it, on the Audit Committee's recommendation where the company has one, and the shareholders ratify it by an ordinary resolution at a general meeting.

What if the cost auditor resigns mid-year?

The Board must fill the casual vacancy within 30 days and the company must file CRA-2 for the new appointment within 30 days of it.
