What we do

What delay costs

  • ₹200 a day late fee until the return is filed, up to the TDS amount for the quarter.
  • Interest at 1% a month if TDS wasn't deducted, and 1.5% a month if it was deducted but not deposited.
  • Penalty of ₹10,000 to ₹1 lakh for not filing or filing wrong details.
  • Expenses on which TDS wasn't deducted or paid can be partly disallowed in your income tax return.
  • Your vendors and employees don't get credit for the tax, and they will come back to you for it.

Frequently asked questions

We received a TRACES default notice. What does it mean?

Usually short deduction, late payment, a PAN mismatch or a challan not matching. We download the justification report, find the cause and file a correction statement, and pay the balance where it is genuinely due.

Our TDS returns for earlier quarters were filed wrongly. Can they be corrected?

Yes, through correction statements on TRACES. We fix PANs, amounts and challan details so the deductees get their credit.

Do we need a TAN to deduct TDS on a property purchase?

No. A buyer deducting TDS on property uses their own PAN and files Form 141. A TAN is needed for regular business TDS.

Can you handle TDS for a small business with only a few vendors?

Yes. Many of our TDS clients have only rent, professional fees and contractor payments. The fee is fixed per quarter.
