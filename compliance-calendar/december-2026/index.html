Compliance calendar for December 2026: every GST, income tax, TDS, ROC and cost audit due date

ComplianceUpdated 11 November 2026By CMA Sudarshan Rudra Jha

December is one of the heaviest months of the compliance year. Along with the usual monthly GST and TDS filings, it carries the third advance tax instalment, the GST annual return, the last date for belated and revised income-tax returns, and the fee-free window for AOC-4 and CRA-4. This page lists every due date for December 2026 in order, with who it applies to and what happens if you miss it.

The five dates that matter most this month. 15 December for advance tax, 20 December for GSTR-3B, and 31 December for GSTR-9, belated or revised ITR, and AOC-4 and CRA-4 without additional fee.

How to use this calendar

  • Add the dates to your phone. The .ics file above adds every December date to Google Calendar, Outlook or the iPhone calendar in one step.
  • Work back from the date. Most late filings happen because the data was not ready, not because the date was forgotten. Close the books for November by the first week of December.
  • Check holidays. 25 December is a holiday, but the PMT-06 due date does not move. Banks and portals are slow around year-end, so do not leave payments for the last day.

Frequently asked questions

What are the main compliance due dates in December 2026?

TDS deposit on 7 December, GSTR-1 on 11 December, advance tax and PF/ESI on 15 December, GSTR-3B on 20 December, and on 31 December the GSTR-9 annual return, belated and revised ITR for FY 2025-26, and AOC-4 and CRA-4 without additional fee.

What is the last date for GSTR-9 for FY 2025-26?

31 December 2026. GSTR-9 is mandatory above ₹2 crore turnover and GSTR-9C above ₹5 crore.

When is the third advance tax instalment due?

15 December 2026. By then at least 75% of the estimated tax for tax year 2026-27 should be paid.

Has the AOC-4 due date been extended to 31 December 2026?

MCA General Circular 05/2026 allows AOC-4 for FY 2025-26 to be filed up to 31 December 2026 without additional fee. It waives the fee only; it does not change the AGM date or the penalty provisions.
