Income-tax Act, 2025: old section numbers and their new numbers, in one table

Income TaxNovember 2026By CMA Sudarshan Rudra Jha

The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026. Most rates and limits did not change, but almost every section number did. If you have spent years saying "80C" and "44AB", you will now see Section 123 and Section 63 in forms, notices and software. This note maps the numbers people use most.

Which numbers apply to you. Income of FY 2025-26 and earlier years stays under the 1961 Act, so the return you file in 2026 still uses the old numbers. The new numbers apply to income of tax year 2026-27 (1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027) onwards. Notices and assessments for old years also continue under the old Act.

The TDS forms changed too: 24Q is now Form 138, 26Q is Form 140, 27Q is Form 144 and 27EQ is Form 143. Form 16 is Form 130 and Form 16A is Form 131. Our Q3 TDS return note has the full list.

Why the numbers moved

  • Fewer, bigger sections. The new Act has 536 sections against more than 800 in the old one. Many small sections were merged, which is why most TDS now sits in one Section 393 with a table.
  • Exemptions moved to Schedules. Many items that used to sit in Section 10 are now listed in Schedules at the end of the Act, so look there for allowances and exempt incomes.
  • "Tax year" replaces previous year and assessment year. Income earned from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027 is the income of tax year 2026-27.

How to use this table safely

Use it to recognise a section quickly, not to quote it in a legal reply. Some old sections were split across two new ones, and sub-section numbering does not always match. Before you cite a section in a notice reply, an appeal or a tax audit report, check the wording in the official text of the Income-tax Act, 2025. A wrong section number in a reply can delay the matter or weaken your argument.

Frequently asked questions

What is the new section for 80C under the Income-tax Act, 2025?

Section 80C of the 1961 Act corresponds to Section 123 of the Income-tax Act, 2025. The list of eligible investments now sits in a Schedule to the Act.

What is the new section number for 87A rebate?

The rebate under Section 87A is now Section 156. Resident individuals with taxable income up to ₹12 lakh still pay no tax under the new regime.

Which section replaces 44AB tax audit?

Tax audit is now under Section 63 of the Income-tax Act, 2025, and the audit report is filed in Form 26 instead of Forms 3CA, 3CB and 3CD.

Do I use the new section numbers in my ITR for FY 2025-26?

No. Income of FY 2025-26 is taxed under the 1961 Act, so that return uses the old section numbers. The new numbers apply from tax year 2026-27.
