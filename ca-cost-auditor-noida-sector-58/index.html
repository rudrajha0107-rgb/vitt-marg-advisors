What we handle for Sector 58 businesses

  • Cost records and cost audit: manufacturers above ₹35 crore turnover often need cost records, and larger ones a cost audit. We check applicability product by product. See cost auditor in Noida.

Frequently asked questions

Where is your office, and do you visit Sector 58?

Our office is at Suite 103, H-36, Sector 63, Noida. We work with businesses across Sector 58 and visit factories and offices for audits, stock verification and meetings as needed.

How many companies are registered in Sector 58?

Based on Ministry of Corporate Affairs data as of 1 November 2026, 181 active companies and LLPs have their registered office in Sector 58, of which 34 are manufacturers. Factories whose registered office is elsewhere are not included in this count.

Does my factory need a cost audit?

It depends on your products and turnover. Cost records start at ₹35 crore overall turnover for covered products, and cost audit at ₹50 crore or ₹100 crore depending on the sector, with product-level limits too. Micro and small enterprises are outside the rules. Send us your product list and last two years' turnover for a free check.

Do you handle both CA and CMA work?

Yes. Tax audit, statutory audit, income tax and GST are handled by Chartered Accountants, and cost audit, cost records, costing and CMA data by Cost and Management Accountants, under one team.
