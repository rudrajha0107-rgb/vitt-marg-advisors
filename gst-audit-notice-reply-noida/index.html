Annual return and GSTR-9C

  • Turnover up to ₹2 crore: GSTR-9 is optional.
  • Above ₹2 crore: GSTR-9 is mandatory.
  • Above ₹5 crore: GSTR-9C as well, which reconciles the annual return with your audited accounts.

The FY 2025-26 annual return is due on 31 December 2026, and it has an extra difficulty this year because GST rates changed on 22 September 2025. Read our GSTR-9 and 9C note.

Departmental GST audit

The department can audit a registered business under section 65 of the CGST Act, starting with a notice in Form ADT-01 and ending with findings in Form ADT-02. We prepare the reconciliations the officers will ask for, attend the audit with you, and respond to each observation before it turns into a demand.

Appeals

An appeal against a GST order goes first to the Appellate Authority, with a pre-deposit of 10% of the disputed tax, and then to the GST Appellate Tribunal. Appeals are handled with Adv. Prabhash Mishra, a tax advocate with more than 15 years in indirect tax.

Why notices usually come

  • Tax in GSTR-1 is higher than tax paid in GSTR-3B.
  • Input tax credit claimed in GSTR-3B is higher than GSTR-2B.
  • Annual return figures do not match the audited accounts or the income-tax return.
  • Credit taken on invoices from suppliers whose registration was later cancelled.

Frequently asked questions

Is GSTR-9C still certified by a CA?

No. Since FY 2020-21, GSTR-9C is self-certified by the taxpayer. The reconciliation still has to be right, and that is the part we prepare and check.

We got an ASMT-10 for a small difference. Should we just pay?

If the difference is genuine, paying the tax with interest through DRC-03 and saying so in the reply often closes the matter cheaply. If it is a timing difference or a mistake in the notice, a reconciled reply usually closes it without payment.

What happens if we miss the reply date?

The officer can decide the case without hearing you, and the demand then has to be fought in appeal with a pre-deposit. If you need more time, ask in writing before the date.

Do you handle GST cases outside Noida?

Yes. Most GST replies and hearings are online, so we act for businesses across Uttar Pradesh, Delhi and other states.
