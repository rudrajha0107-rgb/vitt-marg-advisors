HRA in 8 cities, Form 124 and bigger allowances: the Income-tax Rules 2026 changes to check before you submit proofs

Income TaxNovember 2026Vitt-Marg Advisors

Between December and February, most employers ask staff to submit proof of rent, investments and other claims so that tax can be deducted correctly from salary. This year the proofs go under new rules. The Income-tax Rules, 2026, made under the new Income-tax Act, 2025, apply from tax year 2026-27 (1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027), and they change several numbers that salaried people use every month.

In one line: HRA at 50% of salary now covers Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Ahmedabad as well, proofs go to your employer in Form 124 instead of Form 12BB, and limits for meals, children's allowances, gifts and staff loans have gone up sharply. HRA and most allowances still help only under the old tax regime.

1. HRA: eight cities now get 50%

Under rule 279, the 50% limit in the HRA formula now applies in eight cities: Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Ahmedabad. Everywhere else, including Noida, Gurugram and Ghaziabad, the limit stays 40%. What counts is the city where your rented house is, not where your office is.

Example. Basic salary plus DA of ₹60,000 a month in Bengaluru, HRA received ₹30,000, rent paid ₹40,000. Exempt HRA is the lowest of: HRA received (₹30,000), 50% of salary (₹30,000), and rent minus 10% of salary (₹34,000). That is ₹30,000 a month. Under the old 40% limit, it would have been ₹24,000, so the exemption goes up by ₹72,000 a year.

HRA exemption is not available if you choose the new regime.

2. Form 124 replaces Form 12BB

If you want your employer to deduct TDS under the old regime, you must declare your claims in Form 124. For rent, it asks for:

  • the landlord's name, address and PAN, which can be left out if rent for the year is up to ₹1 lakh;
  • the landlord's Aadhaar number;
  • the rent paid and the rent agreement; and
  • your relationship with the landlord, which is new. Rent paid to parents is still allowed if it is genuine, but it now has to be declared openly and backed by a written agreement and actual payment.

Form 16 has also been renumbered as Form 130.

Two cautions. Gifts are a threshold: once they reach ₹15,000 in a year, the whole amount is taxed, and cash or gift cheques are taxed even below it. And the children's allowances, like HRA, help only under the old regime.

This raises the taxable salary under both regimes, so expect slightly higher TDS from the months in which payroll applies the new figures.

Checklist before you submit proofs

  • Decide your regime first. If the new regime gives lower tax, most of the proofs below are not needed.
  • Rent receipts, the rent agreement and the landlord's PAN and Aadhaar, ready for Form 124.
  • Proof of deductions under the old regime, such as PPF, ELSS, life insurance, home loan interest and health insurance. Under the new Act, 80C is section 123 and 80D is section 126.
  • Ask payroll whether meal vouchers and children's allowances have been updated to the new limits.

Frequently asked questions

Which cities get 50% HRA from 2026-27?

Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Ahmedabad. All other cities, including Noida and Gurugram, get 40%.

What is Form 124?

Form 124 under the Income-tax Rules, 2026 replaces Form 12BB. Employees use it to declare HRA, deductions and other claims to the employer for TDS under the old regime.

Is the landlord's PAN needed for HRA in 2026?

Yes, if total rent in the year is more than ₹1 lakh. Form 124 also asks for the landlord's Aadhaar and your relationship with the landlord.

What is the tax-free limit for meal vouchers from 2026-27?

₹200 a meal for food and non-alcoholic drinks provided during office hours, up from ₹50.

Do HRA and children's education allowance help under the new tax regime?

No. They reduce tax only under the old regime. The perquisite value of a company car, however, is taxed under both regimes.
