Statutory audit due date for FY 2025-26: the timeline for companies and LLPs

AuditNovember 2026By CMA Sudarshan Rudra Jha

The Companies Act does not give a separate "last date" for the statutory audit. Instead, the audit has to be finished in time for the company's Annual General Meeting (AGM), because the audited accounts are approved at the AGM. So the real deadline is the AGM date.

Company: the FY 2025-26 timeline

StepDue date
Accounts finalised and statutory audit report signedBefore the AGM
AGM (within 6 months of year-end)30 September 2026
First AGM of a new companyWithin 9 months of the end of its first financial year
AOC-4 (financial statements to ROC)Within 30 days of the AGM
MGT-7 or MGT-7A (annual return)Within 60 days of the AGM
ADT-1 (auditor appointment, if new auditor)Within 15 days of the AGM

If the company also needs a tax audit, the same audited accounts are used for it, so most companies finish the statutory audit by August or September.

LLP: when is an audit needed and when is it due

An LLP needs an audit only if its turnover is more than ₹40 lakh or the partners' contribution is more than ₹25 lakh. The audited accounts go to the ROC in Form 8 by 30 October. The annual return, Form 11, is due by 30 May.

Already late? Here is what to do

  • AGM not held: the ROC can extend the AGM by up to 3 months if you apply in Form GNL-1 before 30 September. If that was missed, hold the AGM as soon as possible. Delay can attract penalty on the company and its officers.
  • AOC-4 or MGT-7 late: an additional fee of ₹100 per day per form applies. Check if any MCA fee waiver is running before you file.
  • Form 8 late: ₹100 per day additional fee for an LLP as well.

The longer you wait, the bigger the fee. Getting the audit signed is the first step, because no annual filing can happen without it.

Documents the auditor will ask for

  • Trial balance, ledgers and bank statements for the full year
  • Sales and purchase registers, GST returns and TDS returns
  • Fixed asset register, loan statements and confirmations
  • Board minutes, share capital records and related party details
  • Stock statement as on 31 March 2026

Frequently asked questions

What is the due date for statutory audit of a company for FY 2025-26?

The audit must be completed before the AGM, and the AGM for FY 2025-26 had to be held by 30 September 2026. So the audit had to be done by then.

What is the due date for AOC-4 and MGT-7?

AOC-4 is due within 30 days of the AGM, and MGT-7 or MGT-7A within 60 days of the AGM.

When is an LLP audit required?

When turnover is more than ₹40 lakh or the partners' contribution is more than ₹25 lakh. Audited accounts are filed in Form 8 by 30 October.

What if the AGM was not held by 30 September?

Hold it as soon as possible and file AOC-4 and MGT-7 with additional fees. Delays can also attract penalty for the company and its officers.
