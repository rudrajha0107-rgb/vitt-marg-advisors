DIR-3 KYC in 2026: it is no longer every year. Here's when yours is due

ROCNovember 2026Vitt-Marg Advisors

For years, every director in India filed DIR-3 KYC by 30 September. That has changed. The Companies (Appointment and Qualification of Directors) Amendment Rules, 2025 (G.S.R. 943(E), 31 December 2025), effective 31 March 2026, moved director KYC to a three-year cycle.

The new rule in short

  • Every person holding a DIN files KYC once in every three consecutive financial years, on or before 30 June of the year after that block.
  • Directors whose KYC was already up to date: the MCA has indicated the next filing is due by 30 June 2028. Nothing was due on 30 September 2026.
  • The e-form DIR-3 KYC and DIR-3 KYC Web have been merged into a single form, DIR-3 KYC Web.

The 30-day rule for changes

The three-year cycle does not mean you can ignore changes. If your mobile number, email address or residential address changes, you must update it through DIR-3 KYC Web within 30 days. The OTPs for every MCA filing go to these details, so an old number will also block your company's filings.

If your DIN is deactivated

  • A DIN is marked "Deactivated due to non-filing of DIR-3 KYC" if the KYC is not done in time.
  • A director with a deactivated DIN cannot sign any MCA form, so the company's AOC-4, MGT-7 and other filings stop.
  • The fix is to file DIR-3 KYC Web with the late fee. The DIN is reactivated once the form is approved.

Check your status in two minutes

  1. Go to the MCA portal and use the "Verify DIN/DPIN/PAN" service, or look at the director list in the company's master data.
  2. Check that the status shows "Approved" and not "Deactivated".
  3. Confirm the mobile number and email on record are the ones you use today.

Before AOC-4 (31 December 2026) and MGT-7 (31 January 2027), check the DIN of every director who will sign. A deactivated DIN found in the last week is the most common reason a filing misses the relief window.

Frequently asked questions

Is DIR-3 KYC required every year now?

No. From 31 March 2026, DIR-3 KYC is filed once every three financial years, by 30 June of the following year.

When is my next DIR-3 KYC due?

For directors whose KYC was already up to date, the next filing is due by 30 June 2028. New DIN holders should check their first due date on the MCA portal.

Was DIR-3 KYC due on 30 September 2026?

No. The September deadline was removed by the 2025 amendment rules, effective 31 March 2026.

What if my mobile number or email changes?

Update it through DIR-3 KYC Web within 30 days of the change, whatever your three-year cycle.

How do I reactivate a deactivated DIN?

File DIR-3 KYC Web with the applicable fee. The DIN is reactivated after the form is approved.
