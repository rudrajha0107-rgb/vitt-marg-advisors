For NRIs

  • Residential status: we check your days in India for the year and the special rules for Indian citizens with Indian income above ₹15 lakh, since your status decides what India can tax.
  • Income tax returns: rent, interest on NRO deposits, capital gains, and claiming refunds of excess TDS, which banks and buyers often deduct at high rates.
  • Selling property in India: the buyer must deduct tax on the full sale price unless you hold a lower deduction certificate. We apply for the certificate based on your actual gain, which can release a large part of your money at the time of sale instead of a year later.
  • DTAA relief: avoiding tax twice on the same income, with the Tax Residency Certificate and declarations the treaty needs.
  • Moving money abroad: up to USD 1 million a financial year from your NRO account, with the Form 146 certificate the bank needs. See our CA certificates page.

For residents with foreign income or assets

  • Schedule FA: every resident must report foreign bank accounts, shares, ESOPs and RSUs of a foreign employer, foreign insurance and property in the return, even when there is no income from them. Missing this can lead to penalties under the Black Money Act.
  • Foreign tax credit: claiming credit for tax paid abroad on salary, dividends or capital gains, with the required form filed on time.
  • Returning NRIs: planning the year of return so foreign income does not become taxable in India earlier than it needs to.

For HNIs and families

  • Capital gains planning on shares, mutual funds and property, including reinvestment exemptions for residential property and specified bonds.
  • Regime choice and surcharge: comparing the old and new regimes, and timing gains around surcharge slabs.
  • Advance tax worked out each quarter, so there is no interest at the end of the year.
  • Family structure: HUFs, gifts between relatives and clubbing rules, done in a way that stands up to scrutiny.

Frequently asked questions

I am an NRI selling a flat in Noida. How much tax will the buyer deduct?

Without a certificate, the buyer deducts tax on the full sale price, not just on your gain. With a lower deduction certificate from the tax department, the deduction is based on your actual expected tax. We apply for it before the sale so the buyer can use it.

I have a small foreign bank account with very little money. Do I still need to report it?

Yes, if you are a resident, it must be shown in Schedule FA. The reporting is easy, and leaving it out is the mistake that leads to notices and penalties.

Do I need to file a return in India if my only income here is NRO interest?

If tax has been deducted at the right rate and your Indian income is below the taxable limit, a return may not be required, but filing one is usually the only way to claim back excess TDS. We check your case before advising.

Can you work with us if we live abroad?

Yes. Most of our NRI clients work with us over email and video calls. Documents are shared online, and we coordinate with your foreign tax adviser where needed.
