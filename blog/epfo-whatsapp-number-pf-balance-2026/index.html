EPFO WhatsApp number 87964 41800: how to check your PF balance and passbook on WhatsApp

Personal FinanceNovember 2026Vitt-Marg Advisors

On 1 November 2026, at EPFO's 74th Foundation Day in New Delhi, Union Labour and Employment Minister Mansukh Mandaviya launched EPFO services on WhatsApp. Within days, "EPFO WhatsApp number" became one of the most searched money queries in India.

EPFO's WhatsApp number is +91 87964 41800. Save it in your phone and send "Hi". Only use this number. Do not respond to any other WhatsApp account that claims to be EPFO and asks for your OTP, UAN password or bank details.

What you can do on EPFO WhatsApp

  • Check your latest PF balance.
  • See your last five PF transactions.
  • View or download your EPF passbook.
  • Get guidance on the requirements under the Pradhan Mantri Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana (PMVBRY).

How to check your PF balance on WhatsApp

  1. Save +91 87964 41800 in your contacts.
  2. Open WhatsApp and send "Hi" to the number.
  3. Choose your language when the welcome message arrives.
  4. Pick the service you need: balance, last five transactions or passbook.

Use the mobile number that is linked to your UAN. The service matches your WhatsApp number with your EPFO records.

If the chatbot does not work

In the first few days, users and news reports said the chatbot sometimes returned an error or took a long time to reply, possibly because of the rush. If that happens, these older channels still work:

Small inactive PF accounts are being settled automatically

At the same event, EPFO started automatic settlement of eligible inoperative EPF accounts holding up to ₹1,000. If your Aadhaar is verified, EPFO will settle these without a claim form after checking your identity and bank account. EPFO expects about one lakh accounts, worth about ₹5 crore, to benefit.

Is PF withdrawal taxable?

Checking your balance is free and has no tax effect. Withdrawing is different. A PF withdrawal before five years of continuous service can be taxable, and TDS can apply on larger withdrawals. If you are planning a withdrawal, check the tax position first.
