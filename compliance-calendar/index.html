Compliance calendar 2026-27: GST, TDS, income tax, ROC and cost audit due dates, month by month

ComplianceUpdated 11 November 2026By CMA Sudarshan Rudra Jha

Every month we publish one page with all the tax and compliance due dates a business in India has to track: GST, TDS and TCS, advance tax and income-tax returns, ROC filings, cost audit and PF/ESI. Each month's page is published about a week before the month starts, with a PDF and a calendar file you can add to your phone.

This month: December 2026

Advance tax on 15 December, GSTR-3B on 20 December, and GSTR-9, belated and revised ITR, AOC-4 and CRA-4 on 31 December.
