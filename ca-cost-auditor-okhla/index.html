Okhla: exports, apparel and offices

Okhla Industrial Area is one of Delhi's main garment-export and light-manufacturing clusters, with a growing number of corporate and media offices. Exporters here live with refunds, job work and buyer audits.

Vitt-Marg is a team of about 30 professionals, including Chartered Accountants and Cost and Management Accountants. CA-led work (tax audit, statutory audit, income tax and GST) and CMA-led work (cost audit, cost records, internal audit and CMA data for banks) sit under one roof, so you deal with one team for all of it.

What we handle here

  • Garment exporters: exports under LUT, refunds of ITC, job-work challans and ITC-04, and costing per piece and per order.
  • Cost records: companies above ₹35 crore in covered products need cost records; exporters earning over 75% in foreign exchange are exempt from cost audit but may still need records.
  • Stock and internal audit: fabric and trims with fabricators, wastage norms, and bank packing-credit stock statements.
  • Tax and statutory audit: by our Chartered Accountants.

Delhi-specific compliance

Businesses registered in Delhi have a few differences from our Noida clients, and we handle both every day:

  • ROC Delhi: companies and LLPs with a registered office in Delhi come under the Registrar of Companies, Delhi (which also covers Haryana), not ROC Kanpur.
  • Delhi GST: registrations are with the Delhi State GST department or the Central GST commissionerate allotted to you; notices and appeals go through that jurisdiction.
  • Professional tax: Delhi does not levy professional tax on salaries, which simplifies payroll compared with some other states.

Frequently asked questions

We export garments from Okhla and have ITC stuck. Can you claim the refund?

Yes. We review your exports and ITC for the last two years and file refunds that are still within time.

Our fabric is with job workers. How do we keep it compliant?

Goods sent for job work need delivery challans, ITC-04 reporting and return within the time limits. We set up the tracking and reconcile it monthly.

Do export houses need a cost audit?

Not if export earnings exceed 75% of revenue, but cost records may still apply above ₹35 crore turnover. We check product by product.

Do you visit Okhla?

Yes, for audits, stock counts and meetings as the work needs.
