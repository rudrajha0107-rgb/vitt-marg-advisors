Frequently asked questions

What is the 45-day MSME payment rule?

If you buy from a micro or small enterprise and don't pay within the agreed period (maximum 45 days), the unpaid amount is not allowed as a deduction until you pay. It affects your income tax, so we track it in the books.

Our bank wants a stock audit before renewal. Can you help?

Yes. We reconcile stock and prepare the statements first, then carry out or support the stock audit.

We're a small factory. Do we need cost records?

Only above ₹35 crore turnover in covered products. Below that, simple product costing still helps with pricing and bank discussions.

Do you visit units in Sectors 1 to 11?

Yes, for audits, stock counts and meetings.
