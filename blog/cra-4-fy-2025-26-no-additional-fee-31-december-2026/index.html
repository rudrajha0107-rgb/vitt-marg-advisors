CRA-4 for FY 2025-26: file your cost audit report by 31 December 2026 without any additional fee

Cost AuditNovember 2026Vitt-Marg Advisors

Many companies received their cost audit report late this year, and some missed the 30-day window for filing it with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs. On 27 October 2026 the MCA gave them a breather. Under General Circular No. 06/2026, a cost audit report for FY 2025-26 filed in Form CRA-4 on or before 31 December 2026 will not attract any additional fee.

The short version. If your company's cost audit report for FY 2025-26 is ready but CRA-4 is not filed yet, file it by 31 December 2026 and you pay only the normal fee. From 1 January 2027 the full additional fee applies again, counted from your original due date.

What the normal deadline is

The cost auditor must hand over the signed cost audit report (Form CRA-3) to the Board within 180 days from the end of the financial year. For a 31 March year-end, that is around the end of September. The company then has 30 days from the date it receives the report to file it with the Central Government in Form CRA-4, in XBRL format, along with its explanation on every qualification or reservation in the report.

So there is no single due date for everyone. The 30 days run from the day your company actually received the report, which is why it is important to record that date in the Board minutes.

What the circular changes

When CRA-4 for FY 2025-26 is filedFee payable
Within 30 days of receiving the reportNormal fee only
Late, but on or before 31 December 2026Normal fee only (additional fee waived)
On or after 1 January 2027Normal fee plus additional fee, counted from the original due date

The relief is about fees for this one year. It does not change the cost auditor's 180-day deadline, and it does not apply to earlier years.

A checklist before you file

  1. Record the receipt date. Note in the Board minutes the date on which the complete, signed report was received.
  2. Match the auditor details with CRA-2. The cost auditor and the firm registration number in CRA-4 must match the appointment you filed in CRA-2.
  3. Answer every qualification. The Board must give a clear, point-by-point explanation for each reservation in the report. A blank or generic reply invites questions later.
  4. Reconcile the XBRL with the signed report. Product-wise figures, the reconciliation with financial accounts and the annexures in the XBRL file must match the signed PDF. A file that passes MCA validation can still be wrong.
  5. Use the current form. CRA-4 was substituted with effect from 14 July 2025, so use the current version on the MCA V3 portal.
  6. Keep the SRN and challan on the audit file once the form is filed.

If the cost audit itself is not finished

The circular helps only if a report exists. If your cost auditor was never appointed for FY 2025-26, or the cost records were not maintained, the problem is not a filing fee but a default under Section 148 of the Companies Act. Read our note on penalties for cost audit non-compliance and speak to a cost accountant quickly, because a late audit is still far better than no audit.

Frequently asked questions

What is the last date to file CRA-4 for FY 2025-26 without additional fee?

Under MCA General Circular No. 06/2026 dated 27 October 2026, CRA-4 for FY 2025-26 can be filed up to 31 December 2026 without any additional fee.

When is CRA-4 normally due?

CRA-4 is due within 30 days from the date the company receives the cost audit report from the cost auditor. The cost auditor must give the report within 180 days from the end of the financial year.

Does the relief apply to earlier years?

No. The circular covers only cost audit reports for FY 2025-26. Late filings for earlier years attract the additional fee as usual.

What happens if CRA-4 is filed after 31 December 2026?

The normal fee and the additional fee under the Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Rules, 2014 both apply, and the delay is counted from the original due date.
