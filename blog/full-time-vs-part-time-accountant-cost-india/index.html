Full-time vs part-time vs on-call accountant: what each really costs a small business

On-CallNovember 2026Vitt-Marg Advisors

The salary is only the visible part of what an accountant costs. Add PF, leave, a computer, a software licence, training, and the work that stops when the person leaves. For a business with 20 to 40 hours of accounting work a month, a full-time hire is often more than it needs.

A worked example

A Noida trader needs GST and TDS every month, bank reconciliation and a month-end summary. That is about two 3-hour office visits a month.

When a full-time accountant makes sense

  • Hundreds of invoices a day, or several branches.
  • Daily cash handling and billing at the counter.
  • Work that truly fills 8 hours a day.

For everyone else, an on-call accountant plus a yearly review by a CA is usually the better mix.
