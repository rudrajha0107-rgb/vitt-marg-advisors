Ghaziabad's industries, and the audits they need

Ghaziabad has one of NCR's oldest industrial bases: steel and metal products, engineering and fabrication, chemicals, plastics, food processing and a large trading community. Many are family-run businesses that have grown into companies with bank limits, and the compliance has grown with them.

Vitt-Marg is a team of about 30 professionals, including Chartered Accountants and Cost and Management Accountants. CA-led work (tax audit, statutory audit, income tax and GST) and CMA-led work (cost audit, cost records, internal audit and CMA data for banks) sit under one roof, so you deal with one team for all of it.

What we see most in Ghaziabad

  • Steel and metal units: cost records once turnover crosses ₹35 crore (steel is in the cost audit rules), yield and scrap tracking, and GST on scrap sales.
  • Stock audits for banks: drawing-power statements and periodic stock audits for cash-credit limits. Physical stock rarely matches books on the first visit; we reconcile it before the bank's auditor arrives.
  • Tax audit: by our Chartered Accountants, including cash-payment, TDS and MSME-payment checks that commonly trip up older businesses.
  • GST: returns, 2B reconciliation, and replies to ASMT-10 and DRC notices, which have increased for metal and scrap traders.
  • Partnership to company: conversion, restructuring and succession planning for family businesses.

One local note

Ghaziabad, like Noida, is in Uttar Pradesh. Companies registered here come under the Registrar of Companies, Kanpur, and GST registrations are with the UP State or Central GST jurisdiction allotted to you. It matters when replying to notices or tracking filings, and it is something we handle every day from our Sector 63 office.

Frequently asked questions

We run a steel unit in Ghaziabad with ₹50 crore turnover. Do we need a cost audit?

Probably not a cost audit (for steel that starts at ₹100 crore overall and ₹35 crore for the product), but you likely need to maintain cost records because turnover is above ₹35 crore.

Our bank has asked for a stock audit. Can you do it?

Yes. We carry out stock audits and prepare drawing-power statements, and we can reconcile your stock before the bank's own auditor visits.

We got a GST notice about scrap purchases. Can you help?

Yes. Send us the notice. We review the supplier position, your ITC claims and payments, and draft the reply.

Do you have Chartered Accountants for our tax audit?

Yes. Tax audits are done and signed by the Chartered Accountants on our team.
