ITR due date 21 November 2026 for audit cases: who it covers and what to do now

Income TaxNovember 2026By CMA Sudarshan Rudra Jha

If your accounts needed a tax audit this year, your income tax return has a later deadline than everyone else. For the year ended 31 March 2026 (assessment year 2026-27), the CBDT first set it at 31 October 2026. Then, through Circular No. 07/2026 dated 28 September 2026, it moved the date to 21 November 2026.

The tax audit report deadline was also pushed, from 30 September to 21 October 2026. That date has now passed. So if your audit report is filed, the only thing left is the return, and you have till 21 November.

Who gets the 21 November date

  • Businesses and professionals whose accounts had a tax audit this year, whether individual, HUF, firm, LLP or company.
  • Companies, whether or not they had a tax audit.
  • Working partners of a firm whose accounts were audited. Their own return follows the firm's date.

Who does not get it

  • Normal salaried and non-audit taxpayers. Their due date was 31 July 2026. If they have not filed yet, they can still file a belated return till 31 December 2026.
  • Transfer pricing cases (those who file Form 3CEB). Their ITR date stays at 30 November 2026.

What happens if you miss 21 November

You can still file a belated return till 31 December 2026, but it costs you:

  • Late fee: up to ₹5,000 (₹1,000 if total income is up to ₹5 lakh).
  • Interest: 1% a month on any unpaid tax, from the original date till you pay.
  • Losses: business losses and capital losses of this year cannot be carried forward to next year if the return is late.
  • Some deductions that need a return filed on time may be lost.

In short, filing even one day late can cost much more than the late fee, mainly because of the lost losses.

Quick checklist before you file

  1. Tax audit report (Form 3CA/3CB with 3CD) filed and accepted by you on the portal.
  2. Advance tax and TDS matched with Form 26AS and AIS. Pay any balance tax through challan first.
  3. Balance sheet and P&L figures in the ITR match the audited accounts.
  4. Partners' remuneration and interest match the firm's books.
  5. File, then e-verify the same day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the last date to file ITR for audit cases for AY 2026-27?

21 November 2026. The CBDT extended it from 31 October 2026 by Circular No. 07/2026 dated 28 September 2026.

Does the 21 November date apply to transfer pricing cases?

No. Taxpayers who file Form 3CEB have till 30 November 2026 for their return.

What if I miss the 21 November 2026 deadline?

You can file a belated return till 31 December 2026, with a late fee of up to ₹5,000 and interest on unpaid tax, and you cannot carry forward this year's business or capital losses.

Is the 21 November date for salaried people too?

No. Non-audit taxpayers had to file by 31 July 2026. They can still file a belated return till 31 December 2026.
