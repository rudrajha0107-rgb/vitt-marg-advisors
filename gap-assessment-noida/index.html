What we do

  • Document review: policies, procedures, contracts and past audit reports.
  • Interviews and walkthroughs with the people who run each process: accounts, HR, IT, sales and operations.
  • Control testing: we check samples to see whether controls actually work, not just whether a policy exists. For IT, this can include VAPT.
  • Risk control matrix: each risk, its control, the owner and the gap, in one sheet. Read our note on the risk control matrix format.
  • Gap report and roadmap: every gap rated by risk, with the fix, the owner, an estimated effort and a target date.

What you receive

  • A gap report your board and auditors can read in 15 minutes.
  • A detailed control-by-control sheet for your team.
  • A 30-60-90 day plan to close the gaps, and help implementing it if you want it.

  1. Free scoping call to agree the framework, locations and departments in scope.

Frequently asked questions

What is a gap assessment?

A gap assessment compares how your company works today with what a standard or law requires, such as ISO 27001 or the DPDP Act, and lists what is missing with a plan to fix it.

Is a gap assessment the same as an ISO certification audit?

No. A gap assessment is a preparation step done before certification. The certification audit itself is carried out by an accredited certification body.

How long does a gap assessment take?

Usually 2 to 6 weeks, depending on the framework, the number of locations and how many departments are in scope.

Can you help us fix the gaps as well?

Yes. After the report we can help write policies, set up controls, run training and prepare you for the certification or statutory audit.

Do you assess internal financial controls for the statutory audit?

Yes. We test internal financial controls and IT general controls so that management and the statutory auditor have a clear picture before the audit.
