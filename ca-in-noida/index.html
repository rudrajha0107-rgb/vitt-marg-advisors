What our Chartered Accountants handle

Vitt-Marg works with a team of practising Chartered Accountants. Work that the law reserves for a CA, such as statutory audit and tax audit, is carried out and signed by them. Cost, compliance and advisory work is led by our Cost and Management Accountants, so most businesses get everything from one desk.

  • Income tax: ITR filing for individuals, firms and companies, tax planning, advance tax, capital gains, ITR-U and NRI returns. See income tax consultant in Noida.
  • Tax audit: books review and the tax audit report for businesses and professionals above the turnover limits, including the move to the new Form 26 under the Income-tax Act, 2025.
  • Statutory audit: company audits under the Companies Act, CARO reporting and audit-trail checks, for private limited companies and LLPs.
  • GST: registration, monthly and quarterly returns, GSTR-9 and 9C, ITC reconciliation and replies to notices. See GST return filing.
  • Notices and scrutiny: replies to income tax and GST notices, scrutiny assessments, penalty proceedings and appeals.
  • Accounting and payroll: monthly books, TDS returns, payroll and MIS, so year-end is a review, not a rebuild.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have Chartered Accountants on your team?

Yes. Vitt-Marg works with a team of practising Chartered Accountants, alongside our Cost and Management Accountants. Audits that the law reserves for a CA are carried out and signed by a CA.

Who signs my tax audit or statutory audit report?

A practising Chartered Accountant from our team signs these reports. Cost audit reports are signed by a Cost Accountant in practice.

How much does a CA charge in Noida?

It depends on the work: an ITR, a tax audit and a company audit are priced very differently. We give a fixed quote after a short call and a look at your last year's records.

Can you handle my income tax notice?

Yes. We review the notice, the return and your AIS, draft the reply and represent you in scrutiny or penalty proceedings. Send us the notice and we'll tell you what it means and the deadline.

Do you work outside Noida?

Yes. We work with clients across Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Delhi NCR and the rest of India, mostly online.
