How we work

Work done before the due date

Most interest, late fees and penalties we see come from missed dates, not from difficult law. So every client's work runs on a due-date calendar.

Talk to a Professional
01

You deal with a principal

The person who takes your brief reviews the work before it is filed and is the one you call with questions.

02

A due-date calendar for each client

GST, TDS, advance tax, ROC and audit dates for every entity are tracked together. Data requests go out early and drafts reach you before the due date.

03

Working papers on every file

Computations, reconciliations and supporting documents are kept on record, so a notice or an auditor's query can be answered from the file.

04

Advice in plain language

We tell you what a provision means for your tax and cash flow, and what you need to do — not just the section number.

Process

How a new engagement starts

The first few weeks go into understanding where things stand. After that, the work runs on a monthly cycle.

  1. 01

    Review of the current position

    We list every registration — PAN, TAN, GSTIN, CIN or LLPIN — and check pending returns, open notices and outstanding demands.

    Week 1
  2. 02

    Books and filings brought up to date

    Ledgers are reconciled with the bank, GSTR-2B and AIS/26AS, pending returns are filed, and the due-date calendar is set up.

    Weeks 2–3
  3. 03

    Monthly compliance

    Books are closed each month. GST and TDS returns are prepared and sent to you for approval before they are filed.

    Ongoing
  4. 04

    Quarterly review

    A meeting to go through the numbers, advance tax, changes in law that affect you, and anything that needs a decision.

    Quarterly
